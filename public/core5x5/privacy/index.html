Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Core5x5 is a strength-training workout tracker for Android and iOS. It helps you follow a 5x5 program, record sets and weights, time your rest, and review past workouts.

Information stored by the app

Core5x5 works without an account or sign-in. It does not ask for your name, email address, date of birth, or location.

The app stores the following information in its local app storage:

  • Workout sessions, including the program, start and finish times, exercises, prescribed sets and repetitions, weights, and which sets you completed.
  • Exercise edits, including custom names and rest durations, and the current rest timer deadline.
  • Your weight-unit and default rest-duration preferences.

These records are used to show your workout history, resume an unfinished workout, restore the rest timer, and remember your training settings. Workout records can reveal information about your physical activity, even though they are not linked to an account.

Collection and sharing

Core5x5 does not send your workout records or preferences to the developer. It has no server synchronization, advertising, tracking, or embedded analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. The developer does not receive or sell your workout records through the app.

Your operating system, app store, or backup provider may process information separately, under its own privacy policy and your settings. The app also lets you open its source-code repository in your browser; visiting that website is subject to GitHub’s privacy statement.

Support and privacy requests

If you email the developer, your email address, any name you provide, and the contents of your message are received through Gmail. Only the developer handles this correspondence. Messages are not forwarded to another service or used for marketing.

Support correspondence is used to answer questions and resolve app problems. The legal basis is the developer’s legitimate interest in providing support (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Privacy-rights requests are handled to meet legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)). Correspondence is deleted 12 months after the request is resolved.

Contacting the developer is optional and is not required to use Core5x5. Please avoid sending workout records or sensitive health information unless needed to explain your request.

App-store diagnostics

The developer may review crash reports, performance information, and download and usage statistics provided through Google Play Console and App Store Connect. These may include crash traces, app and operating-system versions, device models, country or region, and aggregate usage figures.

Google and Apple collect and provide this information under their platform privacy rules and applicable data-sharing settings. The developer uses these reports to find bugs and improve app reliability. Where a report contains personal information, the legal basis for this use is the developer’s legitimate interest in maintaining the app (Article 6(1)(f)).

Reports are viewed only in the store consoles. The developer does not download or keep separate copies. Report availability and retention within those services are controlled by Google and Apple.

Device backups

Local storage does not mean that app data can never leave your device. Android backup is enabled, and iOS app data is not excluded from device backups. Depending on your device and settings, workouts and preferences may be included in cloud backups, computer backups, or a transfer to another device.

Core5x5 does not operate those backup services. Backup storage, retention, deletion, and any transfers outside the European Economic Area are governed by the provider’s terms and your settings. See Google’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy, where those services are used.

You can also contact the developer about these arrangements.

Retention and deletion

Workouts and preferences remain in local app storage until that storage is removed. Core5x5 does not automatically expire saved workouts. There is currently no in-app control to delete individual workouts, reset all history, or export your records.

On Android, you can remove local records by clearing Core5x5’s app storage in system settings or uninstalling the app. On iOS, delete the app to remove its local records; offloading the app keeps its data.

Removing local app data does not necessarily remove copies in existing backups. Manage those copies through your device or backup provider. A backup may restore previous records when you reinstall the app or set up another device. The developer cannot access or delete your device backups.

Security

Workout records and preferences are stored in the app’s private storage, protected by the operating system’s app isolation and device security. Core5x5 does not add a separate password or its own database encryption. Protect your device and backup accounts with appropriate access controls. No storage method can guarantee complete security.

Your privacy rights

Where the GDPR applies to personal information held by the developer, you can request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of its use. You can also request portability where its legal conditions are met, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

The developer has no remote access to the workout records in your app. You can review saved sessions in History and remove local records as described above. The absence of an in-app export or deletion feature does not remove any applicable legal rights.

Send requests to the email address below. The developer will normally respond within one month. If an extension is permitted and needed, you will be told within that month.

You can complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman or another competent supervisory authority, including in the EU country where you live or work.

Children’s privacy

Core5x5 is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children and does not ask for age information or create user profiles. If a child sends personal information to the developer, a parent or guardian can contact the developer to request its deletion.
